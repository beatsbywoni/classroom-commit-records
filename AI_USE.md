# Use of generative AI in this research (ACM Policy on Authorship disclosure)

**Tool.** Claude (Anthropic), used through the Claude desktop application with file-system access to the project folder. Sessions in August 2026 (collection, registered pipeline, first manuscript) and October 2026 (diagnosis of the registered analysis, corrected pipeline, this manuscript). Model identifiers as recorded by the application: October 2026 sessions, Claude Fable 5.1 and, for the final revision on October 30, Claude Opus 5.5 (the application allows the model to be switched within a session); August 2026 sessions, [author to confirm from session records].

**Roles the model played.**
1. Drafting analysis and collection scripts (`scripts/00`–`40` and `reml_ri.py`) from the author's written specifications (the preregistration text, the implementation-decision log, and the kickoff and work-order documents kept in the project folder), including the regular expressions, model formulas and plotting code. The author reviewed each script, ran it, and compared its outputs against the specification.
2. Running the scripts under the author's supervision and summarizing their outputs: collection and extraction on the author's machine; analyses of the derived tables (repository-level signals and commit-level tables with hashed authors) also in the application's cloud workspace, with results copied back to the project folder; and, through a browser extension on the author's computer, reading the public GitHub pages of the repositories sampled for the check of the assignment-copy rule (`34_verify_copies.py`; only the counts were recorded).
3. Drafting and editing manuscript prose from the author's outline, findings and argument, and formatting LaTeX.
4. Auditing: on October 30, 2026, separate sessions of the model that had not written the analysis were given the scripts, result files and manuscript and asked to check every number and claim. They found a parameterization error in the within-organization trailer difference (E12) and that the L-BFGS optimizer returned, under the label of a random-intercept fit, the coefficients of the fixed-effects solution (E13). Each finding was reproduced against the data before it was acted on. The revision recorded as E14 followed the author's own reviews (sixth and seventh work orders), and the final manuscript was audited in the same way before submission.
5. Making the first-pass judgments of the manual reviews of the activity rules (120 commits for the previous rule and 144 commits, stratified by rule outcome, creation period and organization size, for the adopted rule), following a written protocol, from commit metadata and subject lines. Each judgment is recorded with the evidence it rests on in an access-controlled file (not distributed because it contains commit subjects) so that the author and reviewers can check it. The author checked the judgments of the stratified review; the judgments of the earlier 120-commit review were recorded only as counts by category.
6. Checking every reference: separate sessions of the model compared each entry of the final reference list with the publisher's record (for ACM works, the ACM Digital Library record read in a browser), Crossref, arXiv version histories, DataCite, or the platform page, and each citing sentence with the cited work, on October 30, 2026; the corrections, the sources consulted, and the items that could not be fully confirmed are listed in `REFERENCE_CHECK.md`.
7. Building the reproduction entry point (`00_reproduce_from_package.py`) and the check of typed numbers (`40_check_manuscript.py`), and running them in new folders; the runs and their results are reported in `README.md`.

**A second tool.** The author's work-order memos reviewing drafts of the analysis and manuscript were prepared with ChatGPT Codex (OpenAI). They were passed to Claude with the instruction to verify every claim against the data before acting on it; the decisions that resulted are recorded in `DEVIATIONS.md` (E9–E15).

**What the model did not do.** It did not choose the research questions, the signals, the thresholds, the sample definition, the models, or the interpretations; those decisions were made by the author and are documented in `DEVIATIONS.md`. Apart from the manual reviews in role 5, it did not label data by judgment; all other labels are regular-expression or rule outputs that are published with the scripts that produce them. No statistical result in the paper rests on a model's assertion: the numbers in the paper are produced by the released scripts, and what can be re-run from the released files, and what cannot, is set out in `README.md`.

**Prompts.** The specifications given to the model are the documents in `registration/` and the author's work-order notes; verbatim chat transcripts were not retained for the August 2026 sessions. The October 2026 session transcript is retained by the author and available to the editor on request.

**Verification of this disclosure.** The author confirms the above is complete and accurate.
